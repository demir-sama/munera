[INTRO (0:00)]
  Munera. Enter the arena. Earn your reward.

[PROBLEM]
  Creators wait on DMs.
  Projects can't prove they paid.
  Spreadsheets, screenshots, promises. Nobody can verify who paid whom.
  Munera replaces trust with escrow, and proof.

[HOW IT WORKS]
  How it works.
  One: the sponsor locks the budget in an escrow vault.
  Two: creators submit their links.
  Three: the sponsor approves the best work.
  Four: the creator is paid instantly, and anyone can verify it onchain.

[LIVE DEMO 1 - Sponsor locks the budget]
  Now, the real thing. Live, on Solana devnet.
  A sponsor posts a bounty: five USDC for each of three winners.
  Sponsor locks the full budget in an escrow vault.

[LIVE DEMO 2 - Creators submit]
  A creator finds the bounty on the board.
  Creators submit a link. One entry each, recorded onchain.

[LIVE DEMO 3 - Approve = instant payout]
  The sponsor reviews the entry and approves it.
  Approval pays the creator from the vault in the same transaction.

[LIVE DEMO 4 - Verify onchain]
  Every payout is public and verifiable.
  Creators build a public, onchain track record.

[FEATURES]
  A live leaderboard ranks creators by what they've earned.
  Profiles link self-declared socials, stored onchain.
  Dashboards for sponsors and creators, built mobile first.
  And complete documentation, from quickstart to contract reference.

[SECURITY & TECH]
  Built on an Anchor program, with a vault owned by a program-derived address.
  Checked math. Sponsor-only actions. No double payment.
  Twenty-two passing tests, and no backend: everything is read from the chain.

[END CARD]
  Munera. Enter the arena. Earn your reward.
  After the deadline, sponsors reclaim any unspent funds.
